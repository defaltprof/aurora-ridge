Odds

The chance of each hand is for one hand played with perfect strategy at 5 coins. It is the same for every hand in multi-play, because each one draws from its own deck. "Yours" counts every hand you have finished on this device, so you can watch real results drift toward the math over time.

How it works

  • A real deck. Every game deals from a freshly shuffled 53-card deck: the 52 regular cards plus one joker. The shuffle uses your browser's cryptographic random number generator, the same kind of source a certified machine uses. Nothing is weighted, and no outcome is decided ahead of time except by the shuffle.
  • The joker is wild. It becomes whatever card makes your best hand, so four aces plus the joker is five of a kind, and the joker can fill a straight, a flush or a royal (a "wild royal").
  • Kings or Better. The smallest paying hand is a pair of kings or aces. The joker with a king or an ace counts.
  • Multi-play. Pick 1, 3, 5 or 10 hands. You see one hand and choose your holds. Every other hand starts with the same held cards, and each one draws its replacements from its own separate deck (with your 5 dealt cards removed), exactly like Triple Play style machines. So every hand has the same odds as a single-hand machine, and the results are independent.
  • Coins and the royal. You bet 1 to 5 coins on each hand. A natural royal flush pays 250 per coin, or 4,000 at 5 coins, so max bet pays back more.
  • Pick your machine. The Game button switches between four real Joker Poker pay tables. They differ only in what four of a kind pays (20, 18, 17 or 15 per coin), but that changes the payback by more than 4%.
  • The numbers are exact. The payback figures were computed by checking every one of the 2,869,685 possible deals and all 32 ways to hold each one, picking the best hold every time. Real players usually give back 1 to 3% more than that through imperfect holds.
  • Hand pay. Like a real machine, a game that wins $1,200 or more locks up for an attendant. Hold the reset button for 2 seconds. The win stays on your credit meter.
  • Play credits only, no real money. Credits, settings and session stats are saved on this device.
