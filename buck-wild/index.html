Paytable

Pays are multiples of the line bet (total bet divided by 40). Matches start on the left reel and need 3 or more in a row. Wilds replace everything except scatter, bonus and jackpot symbols. The moon wild is a normal wild. Sunrise wilds grow.

Scatter and jackpot pays are multiples of the total bet.

The 15 lines

How it works

  • The outcome is decided first. Each reel has a fixed strip of symbols. The game picks a random stop on every strip, reads 3 symbols from each, and only then plays the animation.
  • Lines. 15 fixed lines, paying left to right for 3 or more of a kind. The trophy whitetail buck pays the most, then the mule deer, the red stag and the doe, then the cards.
  • Wild. The golden antler rack lands on reels 2, 3 and 4 and stands in for every symbol except shells and salt.
  • Free spins. Three or more shotgun shells anywhere award 8 free spins, and every win in them pays triple. Three more shells during the feature add 8 more. The free spin reels carry more wilds and more deer.
  • Salt Lick bonus. Six or more salt licks anywhere start it. Every salt shows a cash prize, mostly small, sometimes a Mini, Minor or Major jackpot. The salts lock in place and you get 6 respins where only salt can land. Each new salt locks too, and the respins reset back to 6 whenever one drops in. At the end you collect every salt on the board, and filling all 15 spots wins the Grand.
  • The numbers. Symbol weights, the paytable and the salt prizes were tuned in the simulator until the return settled near 96.5%. Free spins and the Salt Lick bonus each come about once every 115 to 120 spins.
  • Art and sound. The deer, antlers, shells and salt lick pictures are original images made for this game with Higgsfield and embedded in the page. Every sound is synthesized in your browser. Credits and session stats are saved on this device. Play credits only, no real money.
