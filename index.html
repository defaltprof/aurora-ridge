Paytable

Pays are multiples of the line bet (total bet divided by 40). Matches start on the left reel and need 3 or more in a row. Wilds replace everything except scatter, bonus and jackpot symbols. The moon wild is a normal wild. Sunrise wilds grow.

Scatter and jackpot pays are multiples of the total bet.

The 40 lines

How it works

  • The outcome is decided first. Each reel has a fixed strip of symbols. The game picks a random stop on every strip, reads 4 symbols from each, and only then plays the animation.
  • Two kinds of wild. The moon wild is a normal wild. It substitutes for everything except scatter, bonus and jackpot symbols and then is gone on the next spin. The sunrise wilds, including the 2x (blue) and 3x (red) ones, are growing wilds.
  • Growing wilds. A growing wild stays on screen while the other symbols spin around it. Each spin it adds one box, in the direction of the gold arrow, which is toward the nearest edge first and then the other way. When the reel is full of wilds it shows for one spin and clears. A multiplier wild multiplies any line it is on, and wilds on the same line multiply together.
  • Free spins. Three or more bonus symbols anywhere award 20 free spins. Two or more during the feature add 10 more. Locked wilds carry into the feature. The free spin strips carry more wilds, which is where most of the extra return comes from.
  • Scatters. A separate symbol. Three or more anywhere pay 5x, 10x or 25x your total bet. They do not start free spins.
  • Animal bonuses. Three meters sit above the reels. Every fox, hare or bear that lands fills its meter, in the base game and during free spins. Each meter fires at a hidden random point, so any single fox, hare or bear can be the one that sets off its bonus: 20 free spins with that animal's twist. When one animal fires, the others can jump in too, and the fuller their meters the better the chance, which is how double and triple bonuses happen. If an animal fires during free spins, its bonus starts as soon as those spins end. The Fox bonus steals every King and Queen off the reels and brings out more hares. The Hare bonus fills the reels with snowflake scatters. The Bear bonus, the rarest, turns every wild into a growing 2x wild and has no diamonds. Jackpots can land in all of them, and the bonus diamonds still give regular free spins on their own. Meter progress is saved on this device.
  • Jackpots. Five jackpot symbols: Mini, Minor, Major, Maxi and Grand. Crowns only land on reels 2, 3 and 4, and three of the same one across those reels pays 12.5x, 62.5x, 125x, 312.5x or 1,250x your total bet. A reel never shows more than one jackpot crown or bonus diamond at a time. Mini and Minor can hit on any spin. Major, Maxi and Grand only land during free spins, where they hit far more often than they could in the base game. In this prototype each tier is its own symbol, which is my guess at how the real game picks the prize.
  • The numbers. Symbol weights and the paytable were tuned in the simulator until the return settled near 98%, which is looser than most real casino slots. Change either and the return changes, so re-run the math lab after any edit.
  • Art and sound. The animal and moon pictures are original images made for this game with Higgsfield and embedded in the page. The other symbols are drawn in code, and every sound is synthesized in your browser, so there are no separate image or audio files. Sound effects are on by default.
