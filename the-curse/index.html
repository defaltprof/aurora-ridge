Paytable

Pays are multiples of the line bet (total bet divided by 5). Matches start on the left reel and need 3 or more in a row. The golden skull is wild on reels 2, 3 and 4 and replaces everything except bones.

Graveyard prizes and jackpots are multiples of the total bet.

The 5 lines

How it works

  • The outcome is decided first. Each reel has a fixed strip of symbols. The game picks a random stop on every strip, reads 3 symbols from each, and only then plays the animation.
  • Lines. Only 5 lines: the three rows and two V shapes. Line hits are rare, but every one pays at least 2x your bet. The horned demon skull pays the most, then the crystal skull, the candle skull and the old skull, then the cards.
  • Wild. The golden skull lands on reels 2, 3 and 4 and stands in for every symbol except bones. Five on a line pays the top prize.
  • Graveyard bonus. Six or more bones anywhere start it, and each bone digs a grave where it landed. You get 6 bonus spins where only bones can land. Every bone builds its spot up one level: an empty plot becomes a grave, a grave becomes a tombstone, and a tombstone becomes a mausoleum. A bone that lands on a finished mausoleum flies to the smallest spot and builds that instead, so no bone is wasted. Three or more bones in one spin add a spin.
  • Bone Wheel. Three or more rolling bone piles anywhere spin the Bone Wheel, the way the buzz saws do in Huff N' More Puff. The wheel is weighted: it mostly lands on cash (5x to 25x your bet), sometimes on a jackpot or the Graveyard bonus, and now and then on the Cursed Graveyard, which starts with 2 mausoleums, 3 tombstones and 6 graves already built. Four piles spin the Golden Wheel instead: every cash slice pays double and jackpots and bonuses come up more often. Five piles, one on every reel, spin the Golden Wheel, double whatever it lands on (a Graveyard bonus pays every prize double) and add a 50x credit bonus on top. The paytable lists the exact chances.
  • The Reaper. When the spins run out, the Grim Reaper sweeps the graveyard three times: first the graves (1x to 5x your bet), then the tombstones (3x to 10x, or the Mini or Minor), then the mausoleums (10x to 50x, or the Minor, Major or Grand).
  • The numbers. Symbol weights, pays and prizes were tuned in the simulator until the return settled near 97.5%. About a third comes from line wins, close to 60% from the Graveyard bonus (about once every 85 spins) and about 9% from the Bone Wheel (about once every 110 spins).
